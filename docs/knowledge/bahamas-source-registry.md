## Dependency Map

This section records relationships between business-formation
requirements and the information produced or required by previous
steps.

### DEP-001 — Incorporation → Business Licence → NIB

Company Incorporation
↓
Business Licence
↓
NIB Registration

NIB registration may require the business licence number and,
for limited companies, the certificate of incorporation.

FoundryAI should therefore treat NIB registration as a downstream
requirement whose inputs can be populated from earlier verified
Business Knowledge.

### FoundryAI Implication

The platform should not ask the founder to repeatedly enter
information that has already been verified or established.

Information produced by an upstream pathway should become available
as structured Business Knowledge to downstream pathways.

Example:

Certificate of Incorporation
↓
Business Knowledge
↓
Business Licence application
↓
Business Licence Number
↓
Business Knowledge
↓
NIB Registration

### DEP-002 — Business Licence Agency Convergence

The Business Licence process is a convergence point for multiple
government requirements.

Depending on the business, FoundryAI may need to coordinate:

- Registrar General / company information
- Trade name approval
- National Insurance Board
- Department of Physical Planning
- Ministry of Works / Building Control
- Department of Environmental Health Services
- Royal Bahamas Police Force
- Bahamas Investment Authority
- Industry-specific regulators

FoundryAI should model these as conditional dependencies rather
than assuming every business requires every approval.

Nova should determine the required approvals from:

- Business structure
- Ownership
- Business activity
- Location
- Premises
- Employees
- Industry
- Foreign / Bahamian ownership
- Regulated status

## Identity Layers

FoundryAI must distinguish between:

### Person / Founder Identity

The individual using FoundryAI.

### Taxpayer Identity

Government tax identity associated with a TIN.

### Business Identity

The individual business/entity being operated.

### Registration Identity

Government registration identifiers such as:

- Company registration number
- TIN
- NIB registration number
- Business Licence number
- VAT registration number
- Other industry-specific licence numbers

### Document Identity

Documents proving the above identities, including:

- Certificate of Incorporation
- TIN registration letter
- NIB documentation
- Business Licence
- VAT certificate
- Other government certificates

These must be linked rather than stored as unrelated uploads.

### DEP-003 — Taxpayer Identity → Business

A TIN represents a taxpayer identity and may be associated with
multiple businesses.

FoundryAI must therefore distinguish:

- Taxpayer identity
- Business identity
- Business Licence identity
- VAT registration identity

These identifiers may relate to one another but must not be
treated as interchangeable.

Example:

Taxpayer

├── Business A
│ └── Business Licence
│
└── Business B
└── Business Licence

## Step 02 — Business / Trade Name

### Agency

Registrar General's Department

### Service

Name Reservation

### Scope

The official government service currently provides name reservation
for:

- Regular Company names
- International Business Company names

Applicability to other business structures requires additional research.

### Eligibility

The government service identifies:

- Licensed Financial & Corporate Service Providers
- Citizens / Residents with a valid credit or debit card

### Process

1. Register for access to the Government Portal.
2. Log in to Registry Services.
3. Select the company type to be reserved.
4. Enter the company details.
5. Enter credit/debit card information.
6. Submit the reservation.

The current government service states that no supporting documents
are required for the name reservation service.

service.

- Immediately if the name is available.

A reservation is valid for 28 days.

The government service states that two extensions of 14 days each
are available.

### Fee

The government service directs applicants to the applicable fee
schedule.

## Step 03 — Company Incorporation

### Agency

Registrar General's Department

### Platform

Corporate Administrative Registry Services (CARS)

### Scope

This pathway applies when the founder's selected business structure
requires incorporation as a company under the Companies Act.

It does not automatically apply to partnerships, sole-proprietor
pathways, or specialized entities governed by other legislation.

### Legal Authority

Companies Act, Chapter 308.

The Companies Act provides the legal framework for incorporation,
memorandum and articles, company names, registered offices,
company records, and the certificate of incorporation.

### Official Digital Platform

CARS

https://pub.rgd.gov.bs/

CARS Business:

https://carsbiz.rgd.gov.bs/

### Verified CARS Capabilities

CARS currently provides:

- Online company incorporations
- Online name reservation
- Corporate amendments
- Annual statements
- Annual fees
- Director and officer information
- Certified corporate documents
- Filing status tracking
- Encrypted document delivery
- Online payments

### Legal Incorporation Process

The Companies Act provides that a company is incorporated when:

1. A compliant memorandum is submitted to the Registrar.
2. The Registrar accepts the filing.
3. A certificate of incorporation is issued.

The certificate is conclusive evidence of incorporation.

### Memorandum

The Companies Act requires the memorandum to contain the information
appropriate to the selected company structure.

For a company limited by shares, the Act specifies requirements
including:

- Proposed company name
- Registered office location in The Bahamas
- Share structure / capital information
- Subscriber information
- Other statutory particulars

### Articles

Articles of association form part of the company's constitutional
documents and must be handled according to the applicable
Companies Act requirements.

### Registered Office

A company incorporated under the Companies Act must maintain a
registered office in The Bahamas.

If the registered office address was not submitted with the
memorandum, the Act requires it to be submitted within thirty days
of incorporation.

### Company Records

The company must maintain records at its registered office,
including:

- Memorandum and articles
- Amendments
- Shareholder meeting minutes and resolutions
- Required notices
- Other statutory registers / documents

### Certificate of Incorporation

The certificate of incorporation is the formal evidence that the
company has been incorporated.

### Required Information

current CARS application rather than inferred.

## Step 04 — National Insurance Registration

### Agency

National Insurance Board of The Bahamas (NIB)

### Service

Employer / Self-Employed Business Registration

### Scope

Businesses and self-employed persons engaging in gainful employment
in The Bahamas are generally required to register with National
Insurance, subject to applicable statutory exceptions.

Registration creates the account used to record National Insurance
contributions and benefit payments.

### Dependencies

**DEP-001 — Incorporation → Business Licence → NIB**

NIB registration may depend on information produced by earlier
business-formation pathways, including the business licence number
and, for limited companies, the certificate of incorporation.

FoundryAI should populate these fields from verified Business
Knowledge where available rather than requiring the founder to
re-enter them.

### Important Dependency

NIB registration is not an isolated piece of information.

For a business, the NIB registration process can require information
generated during earlier business-formation steps, including:

- Business name
- Trading name

## Step 08 — Liquor Establishment Registration

### Agency

Department of Inland Revenue (DIR)

### Service

Liquor Establishment Registration / Certificate of Registration

### Trigger

This pathway becomes applicable when a business sells, exposes, or

Liquor establishments include, among others:

- Liquor stores
- Bars
- Restaurants serving alcohol
- Hotels serving alcohol
- Members clubs
- Nightclubs

The current Business Licence (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2025 defines
a liquor establishment broadly as a business that sells, exposes or

### Legal Authority

Business Licence Act, 2023

Business Licence (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2025

Business Licence Regulations, 2023

### Critical Current Rule

An applicant for the grant or renewal of an annual Business Licence
for a liquor establishment must first apply for registration of the
liquor establishment and obtain a Certificate of Registration.

The Certificate of Registration is therefore a prerequisite to the
annual Business Licence for applicable liquor establishments.

### Registration Timing

New liquor establishments must submit their registration application
no later than 90 days before applying for the annual Business Licence.

Existing liquor establishments seeking renewal must submit their
registration renewal application by December 31.

### Registration Output

Successful registration results in a:

**Certificate of Registration**

The Certificate is associated with the specific liquor
establishment and its registered premises.

A separate Certificate of Registration is required for each outlet /
registered premises.

The Certificate itself does NOT authorize operation.

The business must still obtain the applicable annual Business
Licence.

### Registration Fee

Liquor Store:

$100

Other liquor establishments:

$50

Fees are payable for each registration / renewal application.

### Registration Process

1. Determine whether the business is a liquor establishment.
2. Identify each registered premises.
3. Obtain an approved trading name.
4. Obtain required agency approvals.
5. Assemble supporting documentation.
6. Submit the prescribed registration application.
7. Pay the registration fee.
8. DIR investigates the applicant and premises.
9. Public consultation / public comments occur as required.
10. DIR determines whether the establishment qualifies.
11. Certificate of Registration is issued if approved.
12. Founder then applies for / renews the annual Business Licence.
13. Certificate of Registration is uploaded with the Business Licence
    application.

### Required Supporting Documents

Current DIR guidance identifies, where applicable:

- Lease or rental agreement
- Proof of ownership / conveyance
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Beneficial ownership declaration

- Royal Bahamas Police Force Fire Inspection Report
- Royal Bahamas Police Force Business Inspection / Licensing report
- Department of Physical Planning approval

These requirements should remain conditional rather than being
presented as universally applicable to every liquor establishment.

### Public Consultation

The 2025 Act requires public notice of a public consultative process
for new liquor-establishment registrations.

The notice must provide at least two weeks' notice and may be
published through:

- A daily newspaper
- Department of Inland Revenue website
- Signage at the proposed location
- Other prescribed methods

Written comments from the public and interested parties may be
considered before registration is approved.

### Approval Factors

The Secretary may consider factors including:

- Criminal convictions of applicant, officers or directors
- Whether the community is already adequately serviced
- Proximity to schools or places of worship
- Suitability of the premises
- Size and condition of premises
- Sanitary condition
- Fire exits / safety
- Previous suspension, amendment or cancellation
- Other reasonable grounds

The registration is tied to a specific location.

Each registered premises requires its own registration.

Changing premises requires additional documentation and a new
registration process.

Registration application may take up to six weeks to process.

Once the Certificate of Registration has been obtained and uploaded
to the Business Licence application, the current DIR guidance states
that the Business Licence application should take approximately
two working days.

These are guidance estimates and must not be represented as

## Step 13 — NIB Employer & Ongoing Contribution Compliance

### Agency

### Trigger

### Contribution Calculation

NIB contributions are calculated against insurable wages and the
applicable insurable ceiling.

Current NIB guidance states that, effective July 1, 2024:

Employee contribution rate: 4.65%
Employer contribution rate: 6.65%

Total: 11.3%

These values must be treated as versioned regulatory data because
contribution rates and ceilings can change.

### Contribution Week

NIB defines a contribution week as Monday through Sunday.

Contributions are calculated based on wages earned during the
contribution week, subject to the applicable insurable ceiling.

### Payment Deadline

Contributions are paid monthly and should be received by NIB by the
15th day of the month following the month for which the
contributions were due.

### First Salary

Employee contributions are deducted from the employee's wages
before payment.

The deduction applies from the first salary payment, including
during a probationary period.

If an employer fails to deduct contributions when due, the
employer is responsible for the resulting arrears.
